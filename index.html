Store Policies & Customer Protection

Shipping & Delivery Policy

At Nida Mahendi Designs, we freshly prepare our 100% organic, chemical-free henna cones in small artisan batches to guarantee maximum stain potency.

  • Dispatch Schedule: Fresh batches are freshly mixed, rolled, and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order placement.
  • Delivery Timelines: Standard Pan-India delivery takes 3 to 6 business days depending on your pincode. Express courier partners include BlueDart, Delhivery, and India Post Speed Post.
  • 100% Prepaid Orders Only: Because our organic henna cones are freshly rolled and 100% preservative-free, we do not accept Cash on Delivery (COD) to prevent transit spoilage. We provide FREE Pan-India Express Delivery on all prepaid online orders.
  • ❄️ Critical Freshness Instruction: Because our henna contains ZERO preservatives or toxic chemical dyes (No PPD), you must freeze the cones upon arrival if not using them immediately. Stored frozen, they remain fresh for up to 6 months!

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Customer satisfaction is our utmost priority. Because fresh henna cones and organic pastes are perishable personal cosmetics, please review our policy:

  • Damaged or Leaking Parcels: In the rare event of transit damage or leakage, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery via WhatsApp at +91 7057167751 with photos/unboxing video. We will issue an immediate 100% free replacement or full refund.
  • Order Cancellations: You can cancel your order free of charge before the batch has been dispatched (within 12 hours of placing the order).
  • Returns: Due to hygiene and perishability, once cones are opened or defrosted, physical returns cannot be accepted.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle your personal information:

  • Information Collected: Your name, phone number, delivery address, and email are collected solely to process and ship your henna orders.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: We NEVER sell, rent, or share your contact or address details with third-party advertisers. Data is shared only with verified courier partners for doorstep delivery.
  • Data Security: Your passwords and account credentials are encrypted and securely authenticated via Supabase PostgreSQL cloud infrastructure.

Terms of Service

By purchasing from Nida Mahendi Designs, you agree to the following terms:

  • 100% Natural Guarantee: All henna powder and cones are prepared strictly with organic Lawsonia Inermis, eucalyptus, tea tree, and natural sugar. Absolutely no PPD, sodium picramate, or chemical preservatives are used.
  • Skin Patch Test: While our organic henna is suitable for sensitive skin, natural essential oils may cause mild sensitivity in rare individuals. We advise performing a 24-hour small skin patch test before full bridal application.
  • Customer Support: For any grievances or questions, contact us directly at ri76shaarif@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 7057167751.
